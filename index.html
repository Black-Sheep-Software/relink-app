BUILT FOR STADIA CONTROLLERS

Your controller has more games to play.

Give the controller you already love a new connection to your games. ReLink brings its original Wi-Fi connection to Windows and Linux through a Raspberry Pi.

An independent project by Black Sheep Software.

THE IDEA

A familiar controller.
A new connection.

ReLink is designed around the controller in your hands, with local play and an experience that stays out of your way.

01 / CONNECT

Power on your controller.

Use a Stadia Controller with its original Wi-Fi firmware and a configured ReLink receiver.

02 / PAIR

Choose your computer.

Pair Windows or Linux with the Pi, then choose where each connected controller goes.

03 / PLAY

Get back to your games.

ReLink sends controller input to your computer while the companion keeps you in the know.
